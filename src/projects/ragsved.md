blurb: Master's thesis. The transformation of a Swedish post-war suburban centre. A study of modernism's faded ideals, social and economic vulnerability, and the overlooked everyday architecture as a critical mirror for the present.
content_body: |
  <p>Rågsved is a Stockholm post-war suburb of the kind we have largely forgotten how to read — long slabs, generous plans, a circular Folkets Hus that holds the centre. It is also a place charged with social and economic vulnerability, and at risk of either erasure or fossilisation.</p>
  <p>The thesis reads the everyday architecture of the place as a critical mirror for the present. It proposes a transformation — not a contrast, not a tabula rasa, but a continuation of the typological grammar that was already there.</p>
